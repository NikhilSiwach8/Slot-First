# SHADOWPLAY — The All Hallows' Paper Theatre

*"Every puppet casts a shadow. Tonight, the shadows pay."*

Game design document, v0.1. The working title still needs a trademark check before it's
used; the alternatives are **Wick & Shade** and **Inkfall Theatre**.

---

## 1. The pitch in one breath

Once a year, on All Hallows' Eve, a travelling **paper-cut shadow theatre** sets up its stage
in an empty field. It has no actors, only jointed paper puppets held together with brass
pins. Their stage light is a single **Lantern**, which moves around the stage. Wherever the
light falls, the puppets throw **shadows**. On this night the shadows are alive, and they
count as real symbols.

You never just watch the reels. You watch **the light move**, and the same board pays again
from a new angle.

### Why it's different

| Usual Halloween slot | Shadowplay |
|---|---|
| Pumpkins, witches and graveyards, drawn in a glossy cartoon or dark-realism style | Backlit **paper-cut silhouettes** with visible brass pins, translucent coloured gels and a hand-made stage. Nobody else in the lobby looks like this. |
| Symbols drop and stay where they land | Symbols **cast copies of themselves** in a direction set by where the light is |
| A cascade replaces symbols | The **Encore** keeps the board and **moves the light**, so a frozen board pays again from a new angle. Players haven't seen this before. |
| Multipliers fall as random orbs | Multipliers are **ink**: when shadows overlap, the ink gets darker and the cell is worth more. Players can see why each one appears. |
| Free spins means "more spins" | The bonus changes the light: a bigger flame, more lanterns, and ink that stays on the stage |

## 2. Core grid and pays

- **6 reels × 5 rows**, **ways pays** left to right, up to **15,625 ways**
- Pays need 3 or more matching symbols on adjacent reels, starting from reel 1
- **Moth** is the Wild. It substitutes for every paying symbol and lands on reels 2–6.
- **Ticket** is the Scatter. In the base game it pays anywhere and triggers the shows; in a
  show, 3 or more add spins.
- **Match** is a bonus-only collector symbol (see §5)

### Symbol set: "the Company"

The cast is original. Every symbol is a jointed paper puppet or a hand-shadow shape.

| Tier | Symbol | Description |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | **The Moth Widow** | A tall puppet with veiled moth wings and a lace mourning gown. Her wings are amber gel. |
| H2 | **The Gourd King** | A pumpkin-headed monarch wearing a crown of melting candle stubs. The cut-outs in his gourd glow. |
| H3 | **The Stilt Wraith** | A sheeted ghost walking on long jointed stilts, bent at the knees. |
| H4 | **The Crow Courier** | A crow in a postman's cap carrying a sealed black letter |
| H5 | **The Familiar** | A black cat whose tail is a chain of six jointed paper segments |
| L1–L5 | **Hand Shadows** | Wolf, Raven, Hare, Spider and Stag, the classic hand-shadow shapes. They are drawn as shadows of real hands, so the low symbols carry the theme instead of being card ranks. |
| W | **Moth** (Wild) | A single luminous moth with violet-ink wings |
| S | **Ticket** (Scatter) | A torn admission stub reading *ADMIT ONE — ALL HALLOWS'* |
| B | **Match** (bonus only) | A struck match with a live flame |

Only **H1–H5 and the Wild** cast shadows. The Hand Shadows already *are* shadows.

## 3. The signature mechanic: **Light & Shadow**

### 3.1 The Lantern

On every spin the Lantern swings on its rail while the reels spin and settles on one of the
four sides of the stage:

| Side | Shadows fall | What it feels like |
|---|---|---|
| **Left** | → right | Best for ways, because it pushes symbols toward the later reels |
| **Top** | ↓ down | Stacks symbols down a reel, so one reel holds more ways |
| **Right** | ← left | Pushes symbols back toward reel 1, which can complete short ways |
| **Bottom** | ↑ up | Stacks symbols upward |

The Lantern also has a **flame level**, drawn on every spin:

| Flame | Shadow length | Base-game chance (initial tuning) |
|---|---|---|
| **Candle** | 1 cell | ~80% |
| **Lamp** | 2 cells | ~17% |
| **Bonfire** | 3 cells | ~3% (the Lantern roars, and the stage turns orange) |

### 3.2 Casting

1. The reels stop.
2. Every **H1–H5** puppet and every **Moth** casts its shadow in the light's direction, one
   cell per flame level.
3. A shadow is a **violet-ink copy of the symbol** and counts as that symbol for pays. A
   Moth's shadow is wild.
4. Shadows **overwrite** Hand Shadows (low symbols) and empty shadow space. They never
   overwrite a puppet, Moth, Ticket or Match.
5. Where shadows from different symbols land on the same cell, the higher-paying symbol
   wins the cell.

### 3.3 Ink: the multiplier system

Ink builds up wherever light piles up:

- Where **two or more shadows of the same symbol** land on one cell, its ink deepens by one
  step: ×2, then ×3, and so on.
- A **Moth shadow** landing on a puppet (or on another Moth) deepens that cell's ink too.
- Ink deepens by at most one step per cell per Act.
- **Each winning way pays the deepest ink it passes through.** A way through a ×2 cell and a
  ×5 cell pays ×5. (Balancing showed that adding the ink along a way grew far too fast once
  ink stays on the stage in the shows; "deepest ink" is also simpler to explain.)
- Ink depth is capped at **×10 per cell** in the base game and **×100** in the shows.

The ink is drawn as a darkening violet stain that soaks into the paper stage, with the
number written in quick brush calligraphy. Players **see the multiplier being made**, so it
never feels random.

### 3.4 Encore: the light moves

This is the hook that makes Shadowplay feel unlike any other slot.

1. **Act I** evaluates the board and pays.
2. If Act I had **any win**, the curtain twitches, an **ENCORE!** banner drops in, and the
   Lantern **moves clockwise to the next side**.
3. Shadows from the old light fade, but **ink that formed stays**. The ink has "dried".
4. New shadows are cast from the new angle and the board is evaluated again. **An Encore
   pays only what the new shadows add**: for each symbol, the ways the puppets reach on their
   own are subtracted, so nothing is ever paid twice.
5. **The curtain rises again only while the applause holds up:** after Act II, each Encore
   must pay at least as much as the Encore before it, up to a full rotation of four Acts.
6. **Full Circle:** if all four sides paid, the Lantern returns to its first side as a
   **Bonfire** for a **Fifth Act**, the "Showstopper Act". This is the crowd-pleaser moment.

The symbols never move. Only the **light** moves. Players lean in at every step because
they can **predict roughly** where the shadows will fall, and that partial predictability
is what makes anticipation feel *earned* instead of random.

## 4. Base game rhythm

| Beat | What the player feels |
|---|---|
| Spin | The reels drop like paper flats sliding into stage grooves, and the Lantern swings on its rail |
| Lantern settles | A creak of wood and a flicker. The player sees *the side* and *the flame* before the shadows fall, which is a half-second of real tension. |
| Shadows cast | Shadows slide across the stage with a soft paper hiss |
| Ink forms | A wet ink "bloom" with a violet glow and a number |
| Win | A footlight chase and a marquee count-up |
| Encore | The curtain jolts, the Lantern travels, and the anticipation rebuilds |

The **Ante** bet mode (§7) tilts the odds toward Lamp and Bonfire flames and more Tickets.

## 5. The Shows (free spins)

Tickets trigger the bonus. Each Ticket tier is a different *performance*, and each one
changes the light, not just the number of spins.

| Tickets | Show | Spins | What changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **The Matinee** | 10 | **Ink dries for the whole show**: every ink multiplier stays on its cell across all spins of the show. The flame starts at Candle. |
| 4 | **The Midnight Show** | 12 | Same as the Matinee, but the flame starts at **Lamp** and the stage starts with **one random ×2 ink cell on each reel** |
| 5+ | **Lights Out** | 15 | Ink stays and every reel starts with a ×2 ink cell, but the flame starts at Candle: **two Lanterns** on opposite sides cast shadows both ways at once. Where the shadows cross in the middle, ink builds very fast. |

### Match: lighting the flame

- **Match** symbols land only during the shows.
- Every Match you collect **raises the flame** for the rest of the show:
  **Candle → Lamp → Bonfire**.
- After Bonfire, each further Match **lights another Lantern** on an empty side, up to four.
  With four Lanterns, every puppet casts shadows in every direction.
- The flame meter is a row of candle stubs along the top of the stage frame. A new stub
  lights up as each Match is collected.

### Retriggers

**3 Tickets during a show = +5 spins.** The stage manager runs on with a fresh ticket stub.

### End of show

The ink is rinsed away, the curtain comes down, and the **Show Total** appears on a playbill
card along with the show's stats: the deepest ink reached, the number of Encores and
whether a Full Circle happened.

## 6. Maths targets (to be locked during balancing)

| Target | Value |
|---|---|
| RTP | **96.20%** for every mode (Stake requires the modes to sit within ±0.5% of each other) |
| Volatility | High (9/10) |
| Hit frequency | ~1 in 3.6 |
| Bonus frequency (base) | ~1 in 230 |
| Bonus frequency (Ante) | ~1 in 115 |
| Max win | **50,000×** stake, with a hard round cap. The Final Bow screen plays when it's reached. |
| Max-win frequency | Within Stake's publication limits, checked by simulation |
| Rounds | **Stateless.** Each bet is one complete book, and shows resolve inside the same round, as the Stake Engine RGS requires. |

**Maths stack:** `@slot-engine/core` (TypeScript) for the game model and simulation, its
optimizer for the lookup tables, and its output files for the Stake RGS. The Python
`stake-math-sdk` is the fallback if anything is missing.

## 7. Bet modes

The Playbill holds every bet mode.

| Mode | Cost | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | 1× | Base game |
| **Extra Wick** (Ante) | 1.25× | Doubles the chance of Lamp and Bonfire flames and doubles Ticket frequency |
| **Buy: Matinee** | 88× | Starts the Matinee |
| **Buy: Midnight Show** | 270× | Starts the Midnight Show |
| **Buy: Lights Out** | 600× | Starts Lights Out |

The buy costs above come from the demo simulations (see [MATH_NOTES.md](MATH_NOTES.md)). The final maths will set them so every mode lands on the
same RTP, and buy modes can be switched off for each jurisdiction.

## 8. UI: the Stage Apron bet bar

The bet bar *is* the front edge of the stage, the apron where footlights sit. It isn't a
strip of floating buttons; it's part of the set.

### 8.1 Desktop layout (≥1024 px wide)

```
┌────────────────────────────────── MARQUEE (win display) ──────────────────────────────────┐
│                 ●●●●  B R A V O   1,250.00  ●●●●   (bulb-chase lettering)                 │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
 ╭─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
 │ [BOX OFFICE]      [ ◂ │ 🎟 0.20 🎟 ▸ ]       ( WICK )        [⟳ REPERTORY] [⚡] [PLAYBILL]│
 │  Balance 1,240.00   Seat Selector       spin button        autoplay  turbo  buy/modes │
 │                                     ◐ Lantern compass                                    │
 ╰──── curved stage apron · footlight bulbs along the lip ─────────────────────────────────╯
```

| Element | Design | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| **Wick button** (spin) | A 120 px circular brass **lantern shutter iris**. At rest a small live flame flickers behind the slats. | Pressing it closes the iris (the spin starts) and the flame flares through the slats. While spinning it becomes **SKIP** (snap shut). **Space** spins. If the iris is pressed during a win count-up, the count completes instantly. |
| **Seat Selector** (stake) | A horizontal **roll of ticket stubs**, one stub per stake level. The selected stub is punched and lit. | Drag, scroll, use ◂ ▸ or the keyboard arrows. The stub prints the bet amount in a typewriter face. Hover shows the max stake. |
| **Box Office** (balance) | A small brass ticket window with frosted glass and a numeric display | The balance ticks down on a spin and up on a win, with a mechanical counter roll |
| **Lantern compass** | A small dial under the Wick button showing which side the light is on and the flame level | Always shows the current light, so the mechanic is readable at a glance |
| **Repertory** (autoplay) | A playbill-list icon | Opens a confirmation sheet: number of spins plus stop conditions (on any show, on a single win over X, on balance down by Y). Autoplay always needs confirmation. |
| **Quick Change** (turbo) | A lightning bolt drawn as a cut-paper shape | Toggles faster reels. It never skips the Encore *reveal*; it only speeds it up. |
| **Playbill** (modes and buy) | A rolled poster | Opens the **Poster Wall** modal (§8.4) |
| **Marquee** (win display) | Theatre marquee lettering on a chase of warm bulbs | The win counts up with the bulbs chasing. Wins smaller than the stake show quietly in plain text, never as a celebration (see §12). |

### 8.2 Mobile portrait (≤600 px wide)

- The stage fills the top **62%** of the screen, and the Lantern rail stays visible around
  the grid.
- The apron sits in the bottom **38%**, with:
  - the **Wick button** at 96 px, centred, inside the thumb zone
  - the **Seat Selector** as a ticket strip directly above it, swiped sideways
  - Box Office at the top left of the apron and the Marquee at the top right
  - Repertory, Quick Change and Playbill as 48 px buttons in a row under the Wick button
- **Landscape mobile:** the apron becomes a vertical "wing" on the right-hand side, with
  the Wick button at the thumb's resting point.

### 8.3 Bet bar states

| State | Look |
|---|---|
| Idle | Footlights glow softly and the Wick flame flickers |
| Spinning | Footlights dim, the iris turns to Skip, and the stake controls are locked and greyed |
| Encore running | The footlights pulse in time with each Act. A small "ACT II / III / IV" label appears on the apron. |
| In a show | The apron changes to the **show skin**: the stake turns into a torn-ticket "spins left" counter, the flame meter appears and the Box Office shows the Show Total |
| Insufficient balance | The Box Office window frosts red and the Seat Selector scrolls to the highest stake you can afford |

### 8.4 The Poster Wall (bet modes and buy)

The Playbill opens a wall of **five theatre posters**, one per mode. Each poster has:

- its own artwork (§ASSET_PROMPTS),
- its cost in large type ("COST 100× · 20.00"),
- a one-line description,
- and a **Confirm** step with the amount shown again before the buy goes through.

The posters can be swiped on mobile. Switching to Extra Wick lights a candle on the
apron until it's switched off.

### 8.5 Accessibility and compliance basics

- Hit targets are at least 44 px, with visible keyboard focus rings drawn as brass outlines
- **Space** spins; ←/→ change the stake; Esc closes modals
- A sound toggle is always visible, and reduced motion swaps every animation for a fade
- Text contrast is at least 4.5:1, and ink multipliers are shown as numbers, not by colour alone
- No hidden gestures: everything works with a tap or a click
- A text pack for **social mode** (Stake.us) swaps "bet" for "play" and "win" for "result"
  wherever Stake requires it

## 9. The Info page: the Programme

The info page is a **theatre programme booklet**. On desktop it opens as a two-page spread;
on mobile it's a vertical scroll with sticky act tabs.

| Act | Content |
|---|---|
| **Overture** | The game in 3 sentences, plus RTP, max win and volatility |
| **Act I · The Company** | The paytable. Each puppet gets a portrait card with its pays for 3, 4, 5 and 6 of a kind; the stakes update live with the current bet. |
| **Act II · Light & Shadow** | An **interactive demo board**. The player drags the Lantern to any side and watches the shadows cast on a fixed example board. This one widget teaches the whole game. |
| **Act III · Ink** | An animated example of ink deepening (×2 → ×3), plus "ink adds up along a way" with a worked example |
| **Act IV · Encore** | A four-frame storyboard of the light moving clockwise, and Full Circle explained |
| **Act V · The Shows** | Three poster cards (Matinee, Midnight Show, Lights Out), Match and the flame ladder, and retriggers |
| **Box Office** | Bet modes and costs, RTP for each mode, max win, and the cap rule |
| **Fine Print** | Every rule written formally: the resolution order, malfunction voids all pays, rounding, and the disconnection and replay policy |

Every number on the Programme comes from the same config file as the maths, so the info
page can never drift from the game.

## 10. Splash and celebration screens

### 10.1 Loading: "The Stage Unfolds"

- A black screen with a single **fuse wick** along the bottom serves as the progress bar.
- As loading progresses, the paper theatre **pops up layer by layer** like a pop-up book:
  the back flat at 20%, the wings at 40%, the proscenium at 60%, the puppets hanging from
  their rods at 80%, and the Lantern at 100%.
- At 100% the wick reaches the Lantern and **ignites** it. The light sweeps across the
  stage, and the cast's shadows flash huge on the curtain for a moment.
- Then **"Tap to raise the curtain"** appears.

### 10.2 Intro splash: "Tonight's Programme"

Three posters stand side by side, each with a looping micro-animation:

1. **THE LIGHT CASTS** — a puppet throws its shadow
2. **THE INK DEEPENS** — two shadows overlap to make ×2
3. **ENCORE!** — the Lantern moves and new shadows appear

A *"Don't show again"* toggle and a large **PLAY** ticket stub sit below them.

### 10.3 Show intros

| Show | Intro |
|---|---|
| Matinee | The house lights dim to amber, a ticket is torn in half on screen, and the show's title is stamped on a playbill |
| Midnight Show | The moon rises behind the paper backdrop and turns everything indigo |
| Lights Out | **Total black for 0.8 seconds**, then two Lanterns ignite at once from opposite sides |

### 10.4 Win tiers: "The Applause Ladder"

| Tier | Threshold | Celebration |
|---|---|---|
| **BRAVO** | 20× | Paper roses tossed onto the stage |
| **BRAVISSIMO** | 50× | Roses, then the curtain sways and the footlights chase |
| **STANDING OVATION** | 100× | Silhouettes of the audience stand up in the foreground, and the camera pulls back to reveal the full theatre |
| **SHOWSTOPPER** | 500× | Every Lantern ignites, the whole stage is lit at once, and the marquee bursts |
| **FINAL BOW** | 50,000× (max) | The puppets bow as one, the curtain falls slowly, and gold leaf drifts down |

Each tier can be skipped with a tap or Space. The count-up always lands on the exact
amount.

## 11. Art direction

| Item | Direction |
|---|---|
| Style | **Backlit paper-cut theatre.** Matte black card silhouettes with crisp hand-cut edges and visible brass split-pin joints, lit from behind by warm lantern light through coloured gel windows (amber, rose, teal). It should feel tactile and hand-made, like a museum-grade toy theatre. |
| Shadows | **Glowing ultraviolet ink**, not grey. This is the visual signature: shadows look alive. |
| Palette | Lamp-black `#0B0A0D` · Paper umber `#2A1E17` · Wax `#F3E3C3` · Ember `#FF7A1A` · **Ultraviolet ink `#6B3CFF`** · Moth teal `#3FD9C2` (sparingly) |
| Type | Display: **Fraunces** (soft, high-contrast serif) or **IM Fell English SC**. UI numbers: **Manrope** with tabular figures. |
| Motion | Paper physics: puppets swing slightly on their pins, flats slide in grooves, and card bends at the edges. Nothing is "glossy". |
| Sound | A wooden stage creaking, the hiss of a flame, a paper rustle on shadows, a music-box and string-quartet score, and real applause samples for the win tiers. There are deliberately **no clocks or ticking** anywhere. |

**Rendering:** PixiJS v8 through the Stake **web-sdk** (Svelte). The puppets are rigged in
**Spine** from layered PNG parts so their joints really move. The shadows are the same
sprite with a violet-ink shader and a slight blur, so a shadow costs no extra art.

## 12. Engagement design: why players keep playing

These are the hooks that create **real anticipation and replay value**:

1. **Readable anticipation.** Players see the light settle *before* the shadows cast, so for
   half a second they read the board and predict. Players who can predict, lean in.
2. **The Encore chain.** One win can unfold into up to five Acts on the same board. Each
   step is a new "will it pay?" moment, so the excitement builds inside one spin.
3. **Visible build-up.** The ink grows in front of the player in the shows, and Match
   flames climb the ladder. Progress is something they can *see*.
4. **Mastery.** Players learn which sides help which positions. The interactive Programme
   rewards curiosity, and engaged, knowledgeable players stay longer.
5. **Choice.** Extra Wick and three show buys let each player pick their own volatility.
6. **Shareable peaks.** Full Circle, Lights Out and the Final Bow are built as moments
   players screenshot and stream. That's the strongest organic marketing on Stake.
7. **Collectible posters (cosmetic only).** A "Programme" sticker for each show seen,
   stored locally in the player's browser. It doesn't change any outcome.

**Lines this game will not cross.** Each of these is a fast route to rejection by Stake
review or a regulator, and it also wears down player trust:

- **No faked near-misses.** Reels and Ticket anticipation show only what the book contains.
  There's no extra Ticket "teasing" that the maths didn't produce.
- **No celebrating losses as wins.** A win below the stake is shown quietly and never
  triggers footlights or applause.
- **No pressure UI:** no "you're so close" messages, no countdown offers, and no loss-chasing
  prompts.
- **Autoplay** always needs confirmation and stop conditions, and turbo never hides
  results.
